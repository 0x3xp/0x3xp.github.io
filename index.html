01About

I work at the boundary between security products and the operating systems they observe.

I approach security work from the implementation level. That means reading the binary, tracing the execution path, understanding the operating system mechanism, building a proof of concept, and testing what a defensive product can actually observe.

AV/EDR Evasion is the center of my research. Around that focus, I work with Windows APIs and native interfaces, system calls, process and memory internals, PE structures, debugging, kernel execution, shellcode, malware development, and adversary emulation.

My offensive work also includes red team operations and penetration testing. These areas give the research a practical context: identify a weakness, validate it safely, understand the underlying mechanism, and communicate the result clearly.
