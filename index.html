About

I prefer to understand security products and operating systems at the implementation level.

My primary focus is AV/EDR Evasion. I study where endpoint products collect telemetry, how Windows execution paths expose activity, and how changes at the API, syscall, process, memory, and kernel levels affect defensive visibility.

Alongside that work, I operate in reverse engineering, malware development, red team operations, research and development, and penetration testing. I build proof of concepts, inspect binaries, develop tooling, and document what the experiments show.

I use public repositories and technical writing to record the engineering behind the work, with an emphasis on reproducible research and authorized security testing.
